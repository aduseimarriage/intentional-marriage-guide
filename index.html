Mr. & Mrs. Felix and Nancy Adusei's Step by Step Journey to a Godly Marriage

A Godly marriage starts with intention, long before “I do” and every day after.

We're just a married couple sharing what God is teaching us, using our own home as the example. Whether you're single, courting, engaged or married, there's a step here for you today, and you don't have to walk it alone.

✓ Totally free. No sign-up.
How to use this page · 1 minute
  1. Choose where you are just below: single, courting or married. The challenges, date ideas and checklist change to fit you.
  2. Do today's challenge, then tap I did this today. A new challenge appears every day.
  3. Tick your checklist as you grow. Each tick opens an encouragement, a real-life example and a Bible verse.
  4. Read what helped us in Our story, our own lessons from courting, long distance and planning our wedding.
  5. Watch your progress in Your journey: your streak, calendar and milestones.
  6. Save this page to your home screen in Chrome or Safari, and come back tomorrow.
