How to use this page · 1 minute
  1. Choose your page below or in the brown menu: Single, Courting, Married or Our story. Each page has its own checklist, daily challenge and date ideas.
  2. Do today's challenge, then tap I did this today. A new challenge appears every day.
  3. Tick your checklist as you grow. Each tick opens an encouragement, a real-life example and a Bible verse.
  4. Read what helped us in Our story, our own lessons from courting, long distance and planning our wedding.
  5. Watch your progress in Your journey: your streak, calendar and milestones.
  6. Save this page to your home screen in Chrome or Safari, and come back tomorrow.
